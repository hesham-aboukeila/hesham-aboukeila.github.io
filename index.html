About

A materials engineer
who connects the dots.

I work across the full materials-development arc, from molecular design and structure-property analysis to processing, prototypes, and application performance.

My background spans academic research, industrial collaboration, technical sales, and engineering education across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. That range helps me translate complex experimental evidence into decisions people can act on.

Professional interests

I am interested in R&D and materials engineering opportunities where I can translate polymer science into scalable solutions for sustainable packaging, circular materials, and advanced manufacturing. My experience spans polymer synthesis, compounding, extrusion, film blowing, structure-property analysis, and industry-sponsored product development.
