Case study 02 · Processing innovation

Removing a crystallization bottleneck.

A nucleating-agent strategy transformed PPeAT from a slowly crystallizing laboratory polyester into a material compatible with practical blown-film processing.

The challenge

Good film properties were not enough.

PPeAT offered promising stiffness, rheology, and biodegradability, but its crystallization half-time was approximately 28 minutes at 40°C. The film bubble could not solidify quickly enough for practical blown-film processing.

My role

I screened ethylene-methacrylic-acid ionomers and acid copolymers, quantified isothermal and non-isothermal crystallization behavior, analyzed the effects of cation identity and additive loading, and connected the resulting kinetics to processability.

Technical insight

Trace quantities of ionomer dramatically accelerated crystallization. The non-neutralized acid copolymer was especially effective, while ionomer performance depended on the neutralizing cation. The optimized formulations preserved crystallinity and mechanical behavior while shortening solidification time.
